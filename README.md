# Formula 1: Drive to Survive

## Overview
Drive to Survive is a documentary series on Netflix that gives a behind-the-scenes look at the world of Formula 1. It focuses on drivers, teams, and the intense competition throughout each season.

##  Why I Like It
- Shows the drama behind the races  
- Highlights drivers'personal stories  
- Makes Formula 1 easier to understand for beginners  

## Key Features
1. Exclusive team access  
2. Real race footage  
3. Interviews with drivers and team principals  

## Popular Drivers Featured
- Lewis Hamilton  
- Max Verstappen  
- Charles Leclerc  
- Lando Norris
